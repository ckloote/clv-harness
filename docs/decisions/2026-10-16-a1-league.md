# A1 league: NBA

Date: 2026-10-16

Status: accepted. It settles open decision 1 of `2026-10-16-a1-scope.md`.

## Context

Gate A1 needs 48 hours continuous on a representative league (DESIGN.md §13). The league must have a clean two-outcome moneyline (§1.3), and Track A's check 1 keeps the 24 h offset (`2026-10-16-track-b-closed.md`). So Novig has to list the league's markets at least a day ahead.

**Evidence.** Novig's unsigned public catalog (`/v3/public/catalog/markets`, `marketType=MONEY`), read 2026-10-16, counts only:

| League | MONEY markets listed | Furthest start listed | Reading |
|---|---:|---|---|
| NBA | 31 | 2026-12-26 | Listed days to weeks ahead, and fuller near game day |
| NFL | 28 | 2026-11-26 | Listed weeks ahead |
| NHL | 2 | the same night | Listed late, and few |
| NCAAF | over 100 | a week ahead | Many games, of uneven liquidity |

- **Every market seen** is `OPEN`, with two outcomes named by team abbreviation, `voids: FMV`, and a fee charged `WHEN_LIVE`.
- **Events** carry the full team names (`"<away> @ <home>"`) and `startsTs`.

## Decision

**NBA carries Gate A1.**
- **It reaches the 24 h offset:** markets list well over a day ahead.
- **It is a clean two-outcome contract:** a game cannot end tied, since overtime is played until there is a winner. The NFL can tie, and NHL markets list too late for 24 h.
- **Its slate stresses the collector.** Nights of 10–15 games, plus the next day's markets from the 24 h lead, put 20–30 markets on each channel at once, which is more than the `stream` bucket can subscribe in one go. This is the scale A1 has to prove.

**The code is league-generic.** One NBA regular season (about 1,230 games) is below `controls.sample_events`, so the live check-1 sample is likely the 2027 MLB season. A1 builds discovery, windows and ingest per league, with NBA first, and MLB joins on the same path before Opening Day.

**The contract** is `docs/measurement-contract-nba.md`, a draft. A1 collects and needs no mapping. The contract must be signed off before A2 links markets to events.

## Consequences

- **The collector captures NBA only, apart from MLB until the postseason ends.** Other leagues are added by their own records.
- **Verify the 24 h reach during the storage pilot.** It records, per market, the time between listing and start. If NBA markets list later than 24 h for a meaningful share of games, that is a coverage fact for Track A's check-1 prespecification, recorded before any statistic. The offset is not dropped.
- **Preseason markets** (Novig lists them) may be recorded, but they are outside the analysis population.
