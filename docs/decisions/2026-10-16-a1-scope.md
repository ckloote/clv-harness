# A1: scope of the live Novig reference connector

Date: 2026-10-16

Status: accepted. It is an outline, and the open decisions below are settled before A1's build starts. Each one becomes its own decision record or an amendment here.

## Context

Track B closed without a validated historical benchmark (`2026-10-16-track-b-closed.md`). The live Novig reference is now the only route to a benchmark, so A1 is the critical path. Gate A1 (DESIGN.md §13) requires:
- **48 hours continuous** on a representative league;
- **forced faults:** a mid-stream socket kill, a process kill, a channel stall, a sequence skip and a resubscribe;
- **for every fault,** the correct `collection_gap` open and close, or a documented unscoreable interval;
- **every resync** beginning from a fresh authoritative snapshot;
- **a report** of storage and depth coverage.

Most of the stream mechanics were built for R0 and V0. A1 is mainly about running them continuously, at slate scale, and proving that faults are handled.

## Scope

### 1. Host

- **A high-uptime host.** The development workstation suspends, and the 48 h gate cannot run on it.
- **Read key only.** The read key goes on that host, and the management key never does (`2026-10-08-single-host-provisioning.md`).
- **Clock health.** NTP offset telemetry against `clock.ntp_offset_alert_ms`, and the alert archived as an `event`.
- **Backups and disk alerts.** The archive and database are backed up, with alerts at `r0.min_free_disk_mb` (DESIGN.md §7.5).

### 2. League and contract

- **Choose A1's league before building discovery.** The contract must be a clean two-outcome moneyline (DESIGN.md §1.3).
  - **NBA:** its moneyline includes overtime, so it is the likely candidate.
  - **NHL:** it qualifies only if Novig's market is the two-way line including overtime and the shootout, not three-way regulation.
  - **NFL:** ties exist, so its moneyline is not cleanly two-outcome.
- **Write the league's own §2–§3 of `docs/measurement-contract.md`** before any scoring (that document's §1). This covers voids, postponements and settlement.
- **Verify in Novig's catalog,** before committing: that the league is listed, the market type, `voids`, and **how far ahead of the start markets open**.

### 3. Capture from 24 h out

- **The live family must be checked at the 24 h offset** (`2026-10-16-track-b-closed.md`). R0 subscribes only from `r0.capture_lead_s` = 3 h before the start, so A1 subscribes each market from listing, or from at least 24 h plus `stream.liveness_max_s` before its earliest plausible start.
- **If Novig lists the league's markets later than that,** check 1 cannot run at 24 h. That is recorded now as a coverage fact, before any statistic. The offset is not dropped (DESIGN.md §9.2).

### 4. Discovery at slate scale

- **The source:** discovery runs on the Novig catalog by league, using event `startsTs`, plus the league's official schedule.
- **What gets archived:** every catalog and schedule response, as R0 does.
- **Overrides:** hand-checked overrides keep `games.toml`'s role.
- **Start resolution is A2's job.** A1 records lifecycle and schedule observations, and does not resolve the off.

### 5. Probe budget

A `book` probe costs 16 tokens against a refill of 4 per second. Probing every quiet market every 15 s is affordable for about three markets, but a slate is 10–15 games on the day of play, plus the next day's markets from item 3.

- **Proposed policy: probe where a price will be read.** A price is read at each `controls.martingale_offsets` time and at each cutoff in `close.buffer_sweep_s`. The probe schedule puts admissible evidence within `stream.liveness_max_s` before each of those as-of times, and spends whatever budget is left on the plain interval. Contiguous deltas still count as liveness everywhere.
  - This departs from DESIGN.md §7.2's fixed interval, so it needs its own decision record, written before the gate run.
  - A4's arbitrary entry times reopen the question.
- **Not without asking Novig first:** spreading probes across several read keys to multiply the per-key buckets. Throttles are per key, but using extra keys to get round them may not be acceptable use. Ask developers@novig.com, and record the answer in `docs/vendor-capabilities.md`.

### 6. Continuous ingest

- **Incremental ingest from sealed segments.** Each run picks up where the last stopped, with the frame citations V0 has. This replaces one game at a time with a loaded-game refusal.
- **Gaps follow DESIGN.md §7.2:**
  - a gap opens at the last trustworthy observation, not at detection;
  - affected books are untrusted until a fresh snapshot verifies;
  - the gap closes only after resynchronization.
- **Live trades.** They carry `tradeId`, so live executions take their ID from the venue. Each is reconciled with the daily file's MAKER row as B1 did. This answers DESIGN.md §15 question 9 at slate scale.
- **Schema.** Only what continuous ingest actually needs, such as an ingest cursor, enters a new stage, with its §8.4 tests. A table nobody writes to yet does not exist yet (§8.5).

### 7. Fault injection

- **Socket kill:** drop the TCP connection mid-stream.
- **Process kill:** `SIGKILL` the recorder. The next session seals the orphaned segment with `unclean_close`.
- **Channel stall and sequence skip:** these can't be caused at Novig. A local WebSocket relay forwards the venue's text byte for byte, and it can hold back one channel's frames or drop a single delta.
- **Resubscribe:** unsubscribe and subscribe one market while the stream runs.
- **Labelling:** each injection is archived as an `event` naming the fault, so replay can tell an injected gap from a real one. The relay never rewrites a frame it forwards.

### 8. Storage pilot

- **Run one week before the gate,** to set `book.full_snapshot_interval_s` and check `r0.segment_max_s`, `r0.fsync_interval_s` and disk headroom against measured volume.
- **Report depth coverage:** how often a ladder is complete to the largest `close.depth_notionals_usd`, and how often it hits the 20-level cap.

### 9. Kalshi

Kalshi joins as an independently stored cross-check, polled as in R0, **after the Novig path passes its own fault tests** (DESIGN.md §13, A1).

## Out of scope

- **A2:** the off resolver for the new league, and the `off_disagreement` report.
- **A3:** the live close family, depth walks and cutoff rules.
- **A4:** Odds API polling and entries.
- **Calibration:** any check-1 statistic on live data. A1 may measure coverage per offset and the number of events a season can supply, which are counts with nothing fitted. Track A's check 1 is prespecified separately, before any statistic, as B2 was.

## Open decisions, in order

1. **League and contract** (item 2), from the Novig catalog.
2. **Host** (item 1).
3. **How far ahead markets list,** and so whether the 24 h offset is reachable (item 3).
4. **Probe policy** (item 5), as its own record, plus the question to Novig about multiple keys.
5. **Gate plan:** the fault schedule over the 48 h, and what counts as a correct gap for each fault, written before the run.
