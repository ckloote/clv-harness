# A1 host: a Raspberry Pi running the recorder only

Date: 2026-10-16

Status: accepted. It settles open decision 2 of `2026-10-16-a1-scope.md`, and supersedes the single-host arrangement of `2026-10-08-single-host-provisioning.md` for the recorder.

## Context

Gate A1's 48 hours continuous cannot run on the development workstation, which suspends (`2026-10-06-r0-recorder.md`). Novig throttles per key, so one key must have one recorder: two processes would starve each other's `stream` bucket. R0 is still recording the MLB postseason on the workstation.

## Decision

**The host is a Raspberry Pi** with an SSD on USB, on wired Ethernet at the same home address as the workstation. That address already passes Novig's IP and companion checks.

**The Pi runs the recorder and nothing else.**
- **Installation:** `uv sync --package raw-recorder` only.
- **Not on the Pi:** `clv` ingest and calibration, and `raw-recorder backfill`, whose fetcher peaked at 6 GB in B1.
- **The workstation does all derived work,** from a mirror of the Pi's archive.

**No timestamp before the clock is synchronized.** A Pi without a battery-backed clock boots with the wrong time.
- **The unit** is ordered `After=time-sync.target` with `systemd-time-wait-sync` enabled.
- **The recorder refuses to open a capture window while the clock is unsynced** (`clock_status()`), and archives `clock_unsynced`. A wrong clock becomes a recorded gap, never bad `recv_ts_ms`.

**Storage.**
- **The archive root** is on the SSD: ext4, mounted by UUID with `nofail`, never on the SD card.
- **Power:** the official supply, with a powered hub where a USB SSD browns out the Pi.
- **The enclosure:** one without known USB Attached SCSI faults, or with a `usb-storage.quirks` entry if the kernel log shows resets.
- **Capacity:** sized from the storage pilot's measured volume, starting at 500 GB or more. `r0.min_free_disk_mb` alerts stay.

**The mirror is the backup and the ingest source.**
- **How it runs:** a systemd timer on the workstation rsyncs sealed segments and manifests from the Pi every 15 minutes, never `*.part` files.
- **Ownership:** the Pi's archive is authoritative, and the mirror is written only by rsync.
- **On ingest,** segment hashes are checked against the manifests, as now.

**Keys.**
- **The Pi receives only the `trading::read` key,** as a 0600 file named by `NOVIG_READ_KEY_PATH`.
- **The management key never goes on the Pi.** Any provisioning happens on the workstation (`2026-10-08-single-host-provisioning.md`, Unchanged).

**Cutover after the World Series.** R0 stays on the workstation through the postseason. A1 is built and tested on the workstation against R0 archives and fixtures. When the postseason ends:
1. seed the Pi's archive with the workstation's;
2. stop the workstation's recorder;
3. start the Pi's.

One archive root then carries R0 and A1, with one key and one process.

## Consequences

- **`deploy/` gains:**
  - a Pi variant of `raw-recorder.service`, with time-sync ordering and the archive on the SSD;
  - `archive-mirror.service` and `archive-mirror.timer` for the workstation.
- **Restore test before the pilot:** ingest 849832 from the mirror into a scratch database, and match its golden trace.
- **A power cut costs only data.** A process kill or power loss is already handled by `recover_orphans` and the `unclean_close` journal. A UPS is optional.
